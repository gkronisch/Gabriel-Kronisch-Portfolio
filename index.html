Research

Papers and method

How I test ideas

Pre-registered, and every attempt counts

Major backtests are pre-registered and logged in a trial registry, so the number of attempts counts against a result. Of 100+ documented strategy tests, about half were rejected on the data, including a cointegration pairs idea.

Working paper · 2023 – present

Lead Exposure and Stock Returns in G20 Countries

Panel regressions linking the Environmental Performance Index's lead-exposure measure to stock returns across G20 countries, and what the relationship implies about costs that markets don't price. Currently being revised.

Senior thesis · Willamette University · 2024

Do Cultural Factors Explain Differences in Investor Behavior between Japan and the US?

An interdisciplinary thesis for the Economics and Japanese Studies majors: uncertainty avoidance, loss aversion and the cash-versus-stocks gap, weighed against non-cultural explanations such as retirement-plan design.
